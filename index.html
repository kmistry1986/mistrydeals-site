The three checks

01

A real discount, not a list price

We check the discount against what the product is actually selling for. A "60% off" that only beats a list price nobody paid fails here.

02

Product quality floor

Four stars or better with enough ratings to mean something, and a review profile that doesn't look bought.

03

Still live when you click

Prices are re-checked hourly and expired deals come down. Every card shows when it was last verified.

What we don't do

We don't take payment for placement. No brand can buy a spot, a badge or a guide entry.

We don't require an account. Nothing on the site is gated and we don't need your data to show you a price.

We don't post every discount. Roughly one in three candidates makes it through the checks.

We don't hide how we're paid. Amazon Associates commission on purchases made through our links, disclosed on every page.
