What this does and doesn't do

This is a manual log, not a feed. It doesn't connect to Amex or Chase, doesn't see your statements, and can't confirm a credit actually posted. It records what you tell it you burned, and it keeps that record across periods so you can see the pattern.

If you want it reading your real statement data, that's a different build — it'd need account access, and it'd be worth doing properly rather than bolting on here.

The lazy way to win this one

The credit is up to $25/month against eligible charges — it doesn't care how many services you stack. A single eligible subscription that already costs more than $25 maxes it every month with zero management.

YouTube TV (~$83) or The Wall Street Journal (~$39) each do this alone. Move the billing to the Platinum, enroll once, capture $300/yr forever.

Stack small services only if you already pay for them. Assembling a $25 pile you don't want is how a credit turns into a cost.

Prices are rough defaults — edit them to match what you're actually billed. Streaming pricing moves constantly and varies by ad tier.

Two traps here

· Peacock and Paramount+ may already be free to you. Walmart+ (which the Platinum also pays for) bundles Paramount+ Essential. Don't pay twice for something a credit already covers.

· Don't pay for Apple TV at all right now — her Sapphire refresh includes a free year if she activates by Dec 31.

Anything not on the eligible list — Netflix, Spotify, Max — earns 3x on her Sapphire. There's no credit for it on the Platinum and the earn rate is worse.

DoorDash — it's $240, not $120

Both Ink Preferred and Sapphire Preferred carry DashPass plus $10/month off a non-restaurant DoorDash order. No rollover.

The catch: only one Chase card can be enrolled per DoorDash account. Share a login and you collect one credit, not two. Separate accounts — one card enrolled on each — doubles it.

Her Sapphire Preferred was overhauled on June 15

· Hotel credit doubled to $100/anniversary year
· New $120 Global Entry / TSA / NEXUS credit — didn't exist before
· Free year of Apple TV (~$156) — activate by Dec 31, 2026
· New 3x on gas + EV charging and 3x on Airbnb / Vrbo
· Fee stayed $95

Two clocks: her 10% anniversary bonus dies Oct 1 (she still earns it on spend until then), and her Hyatt ratio drops to 4:3 the same day.

Free money on the Boundless, expiring Dec 31

2026 only: a $100 airline statement credit, $50 per half-year after $250 spent directly with airlines. Existing cardholders must activate manually. If she got the card before Jan 7 and never clicked, she's earned nothing. You fly three airlines — $250 per half is trivial.

Your points pool across the household

Chase lets you move Ultimate Rewards between cardholders in the same household. Her Sapphire and your Ink Preferred are both transfer hubs — so Freedom, Freedom Unlimited, and Ink Cash points can flow to either and out to partners. Whoever has the better redemption in front of them holds the points.

Amazon points are a dead end

The Prime Visa's 5% at Amazon, Whole Foods, and Amazon Fresh is unbeatable. But its points aren't Ultimate Rewards — can't pool, can't transfer, locked at 1¢, and the base rate is 1%. Use it inside the Amazon ecosystem and nowhere else.

Hyatt at 1:1 leaves the household on Oct 1

Both Ink Preferred and Sapphire Preferred drop to 4:3 — 1,000 UR becomes 750 Hyatt. Only the Sapphire Reserve keeps 1:1, and nobody has one. If Hyatt is real to how you travel, that's a $795 decision to make before October. If it isn't, let the ratio fall.

Southwest Companion Pass — the biggest prize on the table

135,000 qualifying points in a calendar year and she flies with you for taxes only, that year and all of the next. Nothing else here comes close.

Transferred Chase points don't count. The path is a timed pair of Southwest welcome bonuses landing in the same calendar year — and with twelve cards between you, 5/24 is a live constraint. Worth mapping before anyone applies.

Verify Before You Rely

Open the benefits page on each account and confirm
  • Every Amex credit needs enrollment first. Resy, lululemon, Uber One, Walmart+, digital entertainment, airline fee. Unenrolled credits don't pay retroactively. This is where most Platinum value dies.
  • Uber One must be set to auto-renew, with the Platinum as the payment method inside the Uber app — and Uber Cash toggled off for the membership fee, or the card never gets charged and no credit triggers.
  • Her two renewal months. The Sapphire hotel credit and the Boundless free night are anniversary-year, not calendar-year — the board can't time them correctly without the dates.
  • Whether the Boundless airline credit was ever activated, and whether the Jan–Jun window is already blown for 2026.
  • Whether you two share a DoorDash account. Determines whether that fix is worth $120 or nothing.
  • Your AA card's post-conversion terms. Aviator Red became a Citi Platinum Select in April. Companion certificate and Wi-Fi credit are legacy perks Citi can pull with notice.
  • Free-bag purchase requirements. United's requires the ticket be bought with the Explorer card. If AA and Southwest work the same way, that overrides sending airfare to the Platinum at 5x.
  • Your combined 5/24 count. Twelve cards, mostly Chase. Know where each of you stands before any Southwest application.
